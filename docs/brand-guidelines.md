# WOW Business and Digital colour and brand guidelines

Proposed version 2.0, 27 September 2026. Based on the supplied `WBD Banner.png`. Use this with the editable brand guideline document before final sign-off.

## Brand expression

**Transformation that works.** Supporting line: **People. Systems. Services. Better outcomes.**

WOW is a senior-led transformation, service redesign and programme delivery consultancy. Associates in business and digital disciplines are assembled according to each engagement. Do not imply permanent employment, a fixed team or guaranteed specialist availability.

The five themes are Strategy and Planning; Service Redesign and Change; Programme Delivery; Digital and AI Enablement; People and Capability. They describe the delivery approach, not separate legal divisions.

Contrast checks: white on navy 16.75:1; white on royal blue 6.66:1; white on teal 6.77:1; dark gold on white 6.06:1. White on flat warm gold is 3.63:1, below 4.5:1 for normal text, so use a darker gold card gradient or navy text. Review contrast at rendered sizes on all pages.

## Type, logo and layout

- Aptos or Aptos Display where available; Segoe UI or a system sans-serif fallback.
- Large, concise headings; readable body copy with generous line height.
- Use the approved logo art and preserve its proportions. The code-drawn site logo must be visually compared against the original artwork before deployment.
- Use the full banner at a readable width, preserving the aspect ratio. The five live text cards communicate its themes on narrow screens.
- Ivory or white main canvas, navy navigation and delivery panels, blue primary actions, restrained gold and teal highlights.
- Prefer authentic images of people and services over generic AI illustrations.

## Content and interaction rules

- Use UK English, plain service language and specific next steps. Keep registration within the Career Accelerator programme journey.
- Attribute previous employer work to Rennie Mudzi, not to WOW company contracts; verify public claims and permissions.
- Zimbabwe work is developing; do not present proposed partnerships as signed or completed.
- Every button needs a working destination. Enquiry and payment messages must reflect actual backend outcomes.
- Provide descriptive links, meaningful alt text, visible focus rings and mobile layouts. Aim for WCAG AA contrast: 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text and important controls.
